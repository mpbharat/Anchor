HOW ANCHOR WORKS

It doesn't just track you. It holds you.

Anchor is a conversation first. You talk to it like a coach who knows your week. Underneath, it is doing six things.

4

Commit to a few

You put three or four things on the record for the week. That cap is the whole point. Anchor guards it.

✕

It says no

Try to add a fifth and Anchor refuses, reflecting your real load back and naming exactly what it weighed against.

◉

It learns you

Every conversation updates its memory of how you overcommit, what you value, and your habits. The more you talk, the sharper its push-back.

↻

It follows up

It remembers what you committed to and checks in. Not nagging. A coach who noticed you said Sunday and Sunday is here.

◔

It stays silent

When nothing needs saying, it says nothing, and logs every silent call so you see everything it chose not to interrupt you with.

🎙

Talk by voice

A live, natural voice conversation. Think out loud, and it grounds its advice in real sources instead of guessing.

NUDGES, IN THE RIGHT DIRECTION

It reads the signals. It nudges when it counts.

Anchor watches your real life, your inbox, your calendar, and pushes a nudge only when something actually breaches or a pattern is about to repeat. On your phone and your wrist. Never noise for its own sake.

REACTIVE · SOMETHING BREACHED

Gmail: an Amazon receipt lands and pushes you past your dining cap. "That's 1,050 of your 1,000 AED. Worth it?"

Calendar: a 3-hour meeting drops onto your deep-work morning. "This collides with shipping the pricing revamp. Move it or drop it?"

PROACTIVE · A PATTERN IS COMING

Learned habit: you order in most Fridays at 6. At 5:45: "Friday takeaway again? You're near your dining cap, cook tonight?"

Learned habit: you overload every Monday. Monday 9am: "Last Monday you added two and dropped one by Wednesday. Ease in."
