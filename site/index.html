#AgentsEverywhere

The agent that says no.

Every productivity app helps you add more. Anchor holds you to three or four things a week and pushes back the moment you try to take on a fifth.

Try it live Watch the demo
Live app running in your browser
WHY IT WORKS

It lives where you overcommit.

Because Anchor sits on your phone with your notifications, calendar, and mail, it sees your real load, not a self-report. A standalone chatbot can't. It has no presence in the moment a commitment is made or broken.

✕

Says no

Try to add a fifth commitment and Anchor refuses, reflecting your load back and naming what it weighed against.

◔

Stays silent

When nothing needs saying, it says nothing, and logs every silent call so you see everything it chose not to interrupt you with.

◉

Talks like a coach

Speak to it by voice. It grounds its push-back in real sources with Exa, so an unrealistic deadline comes with evidence.
